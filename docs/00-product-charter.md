# Product Charter

## Vision

A tiny personal operating system for money.

The product should reduce financial administration, not create more administration.

## User
One owner. No public product launch planned.

## Design principle
**Less interface, more clarity.**

The app should prioritize:
- automatic data;
- clear current position;
- trend/evolution;
- few high-value decisions.

## Primary questions

### Daily
- How much money do I have?
- How much is actually free?
- What changed?
- Am I spending too much?

### Monthly
- How much came in?
- How much did I spend?
- How much did I invest?
- Is my net worth increasing?
- Which categories are accelerating?
- Did I hit the month's saving/investing goal?

### Annual / fiscal
- Which receipts are taxable?
- What was exempt/non-taxable?
- What requires tax review?
- How much IR was withheld/paid?
- Which deductible expenses were recorded?
- What were my year-end financial balances?
- Is the annual declaration dossier complete?

## Product success

The app is successful if the owner:
- rarely enters bank transactions manually;
- reviews unknown classifications quickly;
- can close a month in minutes;
- can understand year-to-date tax position;
- reaches annual tax-return season with organized summaries instead of reconstructing the year.

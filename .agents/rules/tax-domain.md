---
description: Brazilian personal tax organizer constraints
---
Never classify an incoming transaction as taxable solely because money came in.

Fiscal classifications are:
TAXABLE_INCOME
EXEMPT_NON_TAXABLE
EXCLUSIVE_DEFINITIVE
DEDUCTIBLE_EXPENSE
TAX_WITHHELD
ASSET_MOVEMENT
NO_TAX_EFFECT
REVIEW

Tax rules must be versioned by calendar year and source.

Do not state that the owner is exempt from filing merely because estimated tax is zero.

All IR values are estimates for organization, not tax filing.
